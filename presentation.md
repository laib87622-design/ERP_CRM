# SEFAR TRAVEL SERVICES
### Next-Gen Agency Operations & Financial ERP

---

## Executive Overview

- **Core Focus**: All-in-one Travel Agency Management
- **Security**: PIN-Protected Financial Actions & Multi-Role Access
- **Architecture**: React + Supabase Realtime Architecture
- **Localization**: Native Bilingual (English / Arabic) Support

---

## 📊 Operations Dashboard

Real-time visibility into agency health:

- **Key Metrics**: Track total Clients, Active Bookings, and Package Inventory
- **Realtime Activity**: Monitor recent booking statuses in real time
- **Task Tracking**: Stay on top of pending operational tasks across teams

---

## 👥 Client Management (CRM)

Streamlined client care and passport tracking:

- **Full Directory**: Filter clients by tag, booking status, or gender
- **Compliance Guard**: Automatic tracking for **Expiring Passports**
- **Quick Actions**: One-click booking assignments and profile edits

---

## 💼 Finance & Invoicing

Complete tracking from revenue to client settlements:

- **Invoice Lifecycle**: Full tracking of Pending, Partial, and Paid balances
- **Export Capabilities**: Instant CSV data exports for auditing
- **Audit Logs**: Transparent payment history tracking cash and bank operations

---

## 🏦 Treasury & Multi-Currency Bank

Full treasury oversight across multiple currencies:

- **Liquidity Monitoring**: Live total liquidity in **DZD**, **EUR**, and **USD**
- **Supplier Debts**: Real-time tracking of active supplier liabilities
- **Account Controls**: Managed accounts (Cash, Algérie Poste, BNA, RedotPay)

---

## ✈️ Package Management

Custom packages for every travel category:

- **Multi-Category**: Trips, Hajj, Omra, Ticket & Hotel Promotions
- **Dynamic Pricing**: Track discounts, room types, and free-child age policies
- **Print Engine**: Instant high-res Arabic PDF brochure generation

---

## 📑 Visa Workflows

End-to-end visa application lifecycle tracking:

- **Progress Tracking**: Real-time progress percentage per applicant
- **Document Management**: Template-driven requirements for all visa types
- **Country Filter**: Quick filters for Poland, Vietnam, Brazil, China, and more

---

## 💰 Employee & Commission Payouts

Automated performance tracking and payroll:

- **Commission Engine**: Automated payout queue for domestic and international sales
- **Base Salaries**: Configurable monthly salary schedule per agent
- **Secure Payouts**: PIN-authorized disbursements written directly to financial logs
